📖 IMD & NCMRWF Weather Regime Definitions
1. Active Monsoon:

Monsoon trough positioned south of normal over central India with strong southwesterly low-level jet (>15 m/s) and negative sea level pressure anomalies.

2. Break Monsoon:

Trough shifted north to the Himalayan foothills. Convection severely suppressed over central India with intense precipitation confined to Sub-Himalayan NE India and southeastern peninsula.

3. Low-Pressure / Depression:

Organized cyclonic vortex with central pressure deficit <1000 hPa, strong low-level cyclonic vorticity, and high-volume moisture convergence.

4. Coastal Rainfall:

Thermally and frictionally driven coastal boundary convergence along the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal coastlines with elevated maritime humidity.

5. Orographic Rainfall:

Orthogonal low-level onshore flow impinging directly on steep terrain (Western Ghats windward escarpment and Meghalaya hills) causing intense condensation.

6. Western Disturbance:

Upper-tropospheric mid-latitude westerly trough tracking eastward across northwest India interacting with Arabian Sea moisture.
